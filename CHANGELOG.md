# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first build

- A new home for every film, show and book: search TMDB and Open Library together, grouped by type.
- Add anything in two taps (+, then a status), or swipe a result right to put it straight on your list. Undo for 5 seconds.
- Library grid with Films, TV and Books, status chips and sorting. Designed artwork when a title has no poster.
- A simple detail page: artwork, year, runtime or pages, UK age rating, director or author, overview, status and remove.
- Removing something moves it to Recently deleted for 30 days (Library → ••• → Recently deleted).
- Five Looks (Cinema, Noir, Bookshop, Neon, Paper), animation and haptics settings, and three app icons.
- Works offline: search falls back to your own library.
- Opening animation, and a hidden Diagnostics screen (tap the version in About seven times).
